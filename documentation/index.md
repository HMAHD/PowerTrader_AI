# PowerTrader AI

Welcome to the PowerTrader AI documentation!

## What is PowerTrader AI?

PowerTrader AI is an automated cryptocurrency trading platform powered by artificial intelligence and advanced price prediction algorithms. It enables users to trade crypto assets automatically based on AI-generated signals and neural network analysis.

!!! warning "Important"
    This software places real trades automatically. You are responsible for everything it does to your money and your account.

## Key Features

- **AI-Powered Trading**: Advanced neural network for price prediction
- **Automated Execution**: Hands-free trading based on AI signals
- **Multi-Coin Support**: Trade multiple cryptocurrencies simultaneously
- **Risk Management**: Built-in safety features and position sizing
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Live dashboard with performance metrics
- **Easy Setup**: Professional installers for Windows and macOS

## System Requirements

=== "Windows"

    - Windows 10 or later (64-bit)
    - 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)
    - 500 MB free disk space
    - Internet connection

=== "macOS"

    - macOS 10.15 (Catalina) or later
    - Intel or Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3)
    - 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)
    - 500 MB free disk space
    - Internet connection

## Safety First

!!! danger "Trading Risks"
    Cryptocurrency trading involves substantial risk of loss. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

    - Keep your API keys private and secure
    - Start with small amounts to test the system
    - Monitor the bot regularly
    - Understand the trading strategy before use
    - Read all documentation thoroughly
